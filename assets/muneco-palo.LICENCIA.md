# muneco-palo.glb — record

- **What it is**: a black-and-white stick figure (cubic head with a black visor that marks the front, trunk, arms and legs as bars). 1.68 m tall, feet at y=0, origin between the feet, facing +z.
- **Who**: Galatea (Numinia house), generated by `scripts/hacer_muneco.py` (Python stdlib, deterministic: two runs give the same sha256; `tests/test_figure_reproducible.py` regenerates it and compares).
- **Licence**: CC0 1.0. Own work of the house; no third-party assets, no real person.
- **sha256**: b6fbdaea4f949aaf773b2cbc1d70d3e0f9cc507d1422db00c817941c29b47224
- **Measured**: 15 296 bytes; 108 triangles; 2 materials (white, black); 10 nodes; 2 clips: `Walking` (1 s, loop) and `Idle` (4 s). Bounding box x ±0.26, y 0–1.68, z −0.11…0.17 (read with @gltf-transform).
- **Clip names**: chosen so the engine picks them up by name (its walk matcher: walk|camina|andar; its rest matcher: idle|reposo|respira).
- **Seen**: previewed in three.js (`assets/preview/visor.html`, capture `muneco-palo-lado-paso.png`: stride seen from the side) and at rest from three-quarters.
- **Tested in the engine** (origin/main 5b2427f6, local test world): loads (200) and walks with the `Walking` clip, 14 at a time. **Not checked**: the `Idle` clip seen; the weight of a world with 14 figures; the official glTF validator (not installed; only read with gltf-transform, without error).
